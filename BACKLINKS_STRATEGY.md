## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Quality > Quantity**
   - 5 high-quality backlinks > 100 low-quality ones

2. **Diversify Anchor Text**
   - "Vayu Packaging Solutions"
   - "corrugated box suppliers"
   - "3D box designer tool"
   - Your website URL
   - "click here" / "learn more"

3. **Natural Link Velocity**
   - Don't get 100 backlinks in one day
   - Spread them out over weeks
   - Looks more natural to Google
