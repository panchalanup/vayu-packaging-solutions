## 1. How to use this pack

1. Open ChatGPT, start a **new chat per post**. Paste the right **master style prompt** (section 3) as your first message.
2. For each slide, paste the slide's prompt block. Generate slide 1 first. For the rest, keep the **same chat** so the look stays consistent. If a slide drifts, add: "Match the layout, palette and type style of the first image exactly."
3. Check the text in every image. AI sometimes mis-spells. If a word is wrong, ask "Fix the spelling of the text to exactly: ..." or fix it in Canva.
4. Crop or resize to **1080 × 1350 px** (section 2). Add your **logo** in the empty corner area (AI does not reproduce logos reliably).
5. Post with the card's caption, hashtags and alt text. Reply to every comment and DM within a few hours.

Posts tagged **[VERIFY]** contain a number or promise you must confirm first (see section 11).

## 2. Global specs

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Feed post size | **1080 × 1350 px, ratio 4:5 (portrait)** |
| Profile grid | Instagram shows a **3:4** crop in the grid. Keep headline and key objects inside the **centre 85%** |
| Carousel length | 5-7 slides (max 20 allowed, but short ones finish more) |
| Slide 1 | Hook. Big headline, one clear promise, "swipe →" hint |
| Last slide | CTA: "Send your product weight + box size on WhatsApp 85116 58600" |
| Export | JPG (quality 90%) or PNG. Under 8 MB. sRGB |
| File names | `A01_s1.jpg`, `A01_s2.jpg` ... (post ID + slide number) |
| Reels | Any carousel can be turned into a Reel: put slides in Canva or CapCut, 2 sec per slide, 9:16 (1080 × 1920) |

**Important about GPT Image sizes:** ChatGPT creates fixed shapes (commonly portrait 2:3, e.g. 1024 × 1536). It will not give an exact 4:5. The prompts ask for 4:5 composition with a safe zone, so you can crop the top and bottom slightly (or extend the background) to 1080 × 1350 in Canva without losing content. If you want, generate in portrait and use Canva "Magic Resize" to 1080 × 1350.

## 4. Caption formula, CTA bank, alt text

### Caption formula (keep the first line keyword-rich, because Instagram reads captions for search)
```
LINE 1: Hook with a keyword (e.g. "Which corrugated box do you need: 3-ply, 5-ply or 7-ply?")
LINES 2-4: 2-3 short value lines (specific, plain, one idea per line)
LINE 5: CTA (pick one from the bank)
LINE 6: 3-5 hashtags
```
Keep captions at 80-150 words. Use line breaks. No emoji as bullets in technical lines. One or two emoji at most is fine.

### CTA bank (rotate)

| Packaging Finder: about 2 minutes, indicative prices | D04, E05 | Confirm with the live tool |

**Deliberately not used (until you verify and approve them):**
- Company statistics (years in business, number of customers, boxes delivered, number of cities)
- Certification claims of any kind, eco or sustainability claims, and food-contact or pharma-compliance claims
- Prices
- Customer testimonials, names and logos
- Any phrase like "No. 1", "leading", "top" or "lowest price"

**Image rules**
- AI-generated images are **illustrative**. Do not caption them as your actual factory or customer work.
- Reject any generated image that shows a third-party brand name, certificate text or fake barcode. Regenerate with "No text on the boxes".
- Never put customer names, phone numbers or CRM data in a post. The only number shown is your public business WhatsApp.
