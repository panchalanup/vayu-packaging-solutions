## 🔗 How This Triggers Website Indexing

### The Process:

1. **You add website URL** → `https://vayupackaging.vercel.app`

3. **Google's crawler follows link** → Discovers your website
4. **Crawler sees:**
   - ✅ Sitemap submitted
   - ✅ Verified in Search Console
   - ✅ Quality content
   - ✅ Trusted backlink from Google Business
5. **Google indexes your site** → Within 24-48 hours!
