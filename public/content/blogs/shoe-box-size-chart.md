# Shoe Box Size Chart: Sneakers, Sandals, Boots, Heels

A shoe box is sized from the packed pair, not from the shoe size on the tag: pair length plus about 2.3 cm, width plus 2.3 cm, height plus 2.3 cm, each rounded up to 0.5 cm. A men's sneaker pair that measures 30 x 19 x 11 cm needs about 32.5 x 21.5 x 13.5 cm inside.

## How the chart works

There is no single list of shoe box sizes that every brand follows. A women's sandal, a trainer and an ankle boot can all be the same shoe size on the label and need three different boxes. So the chart does what a box maker does: start from the **packed pair** and add room.

1. **Pack the pair the way a customer will see it.** Heel to toe, toe to heel or side by side, with the tissue, stuffing and any shoe bag you use.
2. **Measure that bundle:** length, width and height in cm.
3. **Add the fit allowance.** The 3D designer's fit checker uses 1 cm of padding on each side plus 0.3 cm of ease, so each measurement grows by 2.3 cm, then rounds up to the next 0.5 cm.
4. **Quote the inside size.** Box makers and the designer both use **inside** length x width x height. See [inside versus outside size](/blogs/box-measurements-guide) if you mix the two up.

Worked example, men's sneakers: 30 + 2.3 = 32.3, rounded up to 32.5 cm. 19 + 2.3 = 21.3, rounded up to 21.5 cm. 11 + 2.3 = 13.3, rounded up to 13.5 cm. Inside size: **32.5 x 21.5 x 13.5 cm**.

## Choosing the style

| Need | Style | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Classic shoe box for shops and storage | **Telescope (lid and base)** | The lid lifts off and the box can be reused. The lid top is one clean print panel for your brand. |
| Shoes sent by courier | Plain **shipping carton** around the shoe box | The outer takes the courier knocks and the branded box arrives clean. |
| Premium or gift footwear | Telescope or **slide box** | A slide box is a tray that slides out of a printed sleeve. |
| Sandals and slippers in bulk | **Book box** or a thin telescope | Low height and quick to pack. |

Read [garment mailers and shoe boxes](/blogs/apparel-footwear-mailer-shoe-boxes) for branding ideas and [clothing, saree and footwear boxes](/blogs/clothing-saree-footwear-boxes) for the newer styles. Put the shoe size and colour on the end of the box, because shops stack them with the end facing out.

## A shipper box around the shoe box

Many online footwear sellers ship the shoe box inside a plain outer carton. For the men's sneaker box above:

- Assume the **outside** of the shoe box is about 1 cm bigger than the inside in each direction: 33.5 x 22.5 x 14.5 cm. Check this on a real sample, because the lid doubles the wall at the rim.
- Add 1 cm of cushion on each side, so 2 cm per direction: **35.5 x 24.5 x 16.5 cm** inside.
- That is 14,351 cm3. At a divisor of 5000, the volumetric weight is 2.87 kg.

[Open that shipper in 3D](/box-designer?style=rsc&l=35.5&w=24.5&h=16.5&ply=3-ply&fl=B&src=blog.shoe-sizes.shipper)

A shipper sized to the shoe box and no more keeps freight down, because [couriers can bill on volumetric weight](/blogs/volumetric-weight-calculator-india), the larger of the actual and the volume-based figure. The divisor varies by courier and service, so check your own rate card.

## A master carton for 6 pairs

Shops and wholesalers often receive shoes in a carton of several pairs. Worked example for six men's sneaker boxes, each about 33.5 x 22.5 x 14.5 cm outside:

- Two boxes side by side across the carton: 2 x 22.5 = 45 cm, plus 1 cm of slack, so 46 cm.
- One box along the length: 33.5 cm plus 1 cm, so 35 cm after rounding up.
- Three boxes high: 3 x 14.5 = 43.5 cm, plus 1 cm of slack, so 45 cm after rounding up.
- Carton inside size: **35 x 46 x 45 cm**, volume 72,450 cm3.

[Open the 6-pair carton in 3D](/box-designer?style=rsc&l=46&w=35&h=45&ply=5-ply&fl=BC&src=blog.shoe-sizes.carton)

Weigh a full carton before choosing the board. In the designer's guidance, 3-ply is comfortable for contents up to about 8 kg and 5-ply for about 20 kg, a planning guide rather than a promise. ## Tips that save money

- **Fewer sizes, not more.** Many footwear brands cover the range with two or three box sizes instead of one per shoe size. Group your pairs by the packed measurements above.
- **Check the biggest pair in each group**, not the average, and test one packed box with the lid on.
- **Mind the lid depth.** A deeper lid hides the shoe better and stiffens the box. The designer lets you change the lid depth.
- **Tissue and bags count.** A shoe bag adds height, so measure with it.
- **Print on the lid.** One clean panel is where the logo goes. See [putting a logo on a box](/blogs/logo-artwork-on-boxes).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the size of a shoe box?

There is no standard. A men's sneaker box is commonly around 32 to 35 cm long, 21 to 23 cm wide and 13 to 15 cm high inside, while a flat sandal box can be 26 cm long and under 10 cm high. Measure your packed pair and add about 2.3 cm in each direction.

### What are the dimensions of a shoe box in cm and inches?

For men's sneakers, 32.5 x 21.5 x 13.5 cm, which is 12.8 x 8.5 x 5.3 inches. Divide any cm size by 2.54 for inches. The chart above lists both for nine shoe types, and the designer accepts mm, cm and inches.

### Is a shoe box measured inside or outside?

Box makers quote the inside size, the space for the shoes. The outside is bigger by the board thickness, and a lid-and-base box is a little bigger again at the rim. Say "inside" when you order.

### Can I ship shoes in the shoe box itself?

You can, but the box may be scuffed or have a courier label across your design. Many sellers put the shoe box inside a plain outer carton sized with about 1 cm of cushion on each side.

### Which board should a shoe box use?

3-ply is the usual starting point for a single pair, as in the designer's shoe box template. Boots, wide stacks or heavy carton packs may need 5-ply. Weigh a packed box, test a sample and ask for a quote on that spec.
