## How this library works

There is no folder of fixed dieline files, and that is deliberate. A dieline depends on three things you choose: the **style**, the **inside size** and the **board** (ply and flute), because the panels are drawn a little larger than the inside size to allow for board thickness when the box folds. A file for one size would be wrong for the next. So the designer draws a fresh dieline for your exact box, and the table below is the quick way in: each preset opens the designer with a style, size and board already set.

Every style in the table has a dieline, whether it is a plain slotted carton or a die-cut mailer, slide box or clamshell. For how to read the lines, see [box dielines explained](/blogs/dielines-explained).

## Get the dieline in four steps

1. **Open a preset** from the table, or open the [designer](/box-designer) and choose a style.
2. **Set the inside size and board.** Type your own numbers over the preset. The [fit checker](/blogs/find-right-box-size) turns product size and padding into an inside size.
3. **Fold the box** with the assembly slider to check the shape. Dragging it to flat shows the blank.
4. **Open the Actions tab** and choose **Dieline (SVG, 1:1)** or **Dieline (PDF, 1:1)**.

![The export panel with the dieline SVG and PDF buttons and the box folded flat](/blog-images/designer/designer-fold-flat.png)
*Fold flat to see the blank, then download the dieline from the Actions tab.*

SVG opens in Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity Designer, CorelDRAW and Figma. The PDF is the safe file to email or print at 100%.

## What a dieline from the library does and does not carry

- **It carries structure.** Cut lines, crease lines, panel labels, the glue flap, and a footer with the board and overall blank size. For carry, produce and window styles, the cut-outs are drawn at their real positions.
- **It does not carry artwork.** Your logo and cutouts are not in the dieline file. Send the share link or spec sheet PDF to show where artwork goes ([logo and artwork guide](/blogs/logo-artwork-on-boxes)).
- **It is a starting point for the maker.** A box plant often adjusts glue flap width, slot width or creasing allowance to suit its machines. The inside size is the number that must not change.

## Hand the dieline over

A short brief for your designer or printer:

> Attached: dieline for our [style] box, SVG and PDF, 1:1 mm. Black solid = cut, red dashed = crease. Inside size [L x W x H] cm, board [ply and flute]. Please keep the dieline on its own non-printing layer, keep text 5 mm inside creases and add about 3 mm of bleed. Quantity [x].

Then compare quotes on the same file. The spec sheet PDF, with three views, the board and quantity, makes quotes comparable ([choosing a manufacturer](/blogs/choose-corrugated-box-manufacturer-ahmedabad)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the dielines really free?

Yes. The designer has no sign-up and no locked features, and you can use the downloaded dieline with any printer or box maker.

### Which styles have a dieline?

All 20 in the table: slotted cartons, telescope boxes, mailers and the other die-cut styles. Download it from the Actions tab once the style, size and board are set.

### Can I download a dieline at a size that is not in the table?

Yes. Presets are only starting points. Type any inside size from 5 to 100 cm a side and the dieline is drawn for it.

### Will a dieline from here fit my box maker's machine?

It is a good starting drawing, not a guarantee. Makers adjust allowances to their machines, so send the dieline with your inside size and board and ask them to confirm.

### Why does the dieline change when I change the ply?

Thicker board needs bigger allowances where walls fold around each other. Always download the dieline after you choose the final board.
