# 20 Box Design Ideas You Can Build in 5 Minutes (by Product Type)

"I know what I sell. I just cannot picture the box." If that is you, this page is a menu. Below are **20 box design ideas**, grouped by product. Each one names the **style**, the **board**, a **starting size** and the **cutouts** to use, so you can recreate it in the free 3D Box Designer in about five minutes and then make it yours.

Every idea matches a ready template in the designer, so for most you can open the template, replace the sample name and be done. Where it helps, there is a "make it yours" twist so your box does not look like everyone else's.

## The five-minute recipe

1. **Open the designer** and choose the style named in the idea (or open the template with the same name).
2. **Type your inside size.** The sizes below are starting points; use your product's real size or the [fit checker](/blogs/find-right-box-size).
3. **Pick the board** named in the idea. 3-ply is a single wall; 5-ply B+C is a double wall for heavier loads. See [wall types explained](/blogs/corrugated-wall-differences).
4. **Add the cutouts** from the Artwork tab (search the name), then tint them to your brand colour. The [cutouts guide](/blogs/how-to-add-cutouts-box-design) shows how.
5. **Replace the sample text** with your brand name and fold the box.
6. **Check the estimate**, then share the link or send the design for a quote.

Sizes are inside length x width x height in cm. Boards read "3-ply E" for a single wall with E flute, "5-ply B+C" for a double wall.

## Fruit and vegetables

Fresh produce needs air and a carry handle. Ventilated styles give you both.

### 1. Mango carton
- **Style:** [Ventilated produce carton](/box-designer/fruit-box) with vents and hand holes
- **Board and size:** 5-ply B+C, 36 x 26 x 14, natural kraft
- **Cutouts:** a mango as the hero, a leaf sprig beside it
- **Make it yours:** repeat the leaf as a quiet pattern on the side faces and put the variety name in large text on the front.

![The mango carton: a ventilated kraft carton with a mango cutout, a leaf sprig and sample brand text](/blog-images/designer/designer-template-mango-carton.png)
*Idea 1 in the designer: the Mango carton template.*

### 2. Berry tray
- **Style:** Open produce tray (HSC) with vents, [open it here](/box-designer?style=produce-tray)
- **Board and size:** 3-ply E, 24 x 18 x 7, white board
- **Cutouts:** strawberry and blueberries
- **Make it yours:** keep white board so the fruit colour is the star; tint your logo to one deep colour.

### 3. Farm basket carton
- **Style:** [Carry box](/box-designer/carry-box) with hand holes
- **Board and size:** 5-ply B+C, 40 x 30 x 22, kraft
- **Cutouts:** a basket and a sprout
- **Make it yours:** a farm name on two sides and a simple "this way up" mark.

## How to pick an idea and push it further

- **Start from the product's job:** air (vents), visibility (window), carrying (hand holes), gifting (telescope, slide or book style), shelf (display tray).
- **Change one thing, not five.** Colour first, then cutouts, then text.
- **Limit your colours.** One or two print colours keep the design clean and the estimate lower. See [how price is worked out](/blogs/box-price-estimate-guide).
- **Try the Surprise me button** for a quick alternative layout; undo if you do not like it.
- **Not sure of the style?** The [style picker guide](/blogs/which-box-style-should-you-choose) matches products to styles.
- **Want to see all templates?** The [template tour](/blogs/box-design-templates-tour) lists all 74.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are these designs ready to print?
They are ready to **show and quote**, not to print. Replace the sample text and cutouts with your brand, check the print checks, and send the design or dieline to your printer.

### Can I change the size of any idea?
Yes. The sizes are starting points. Enter your own inside size, or let the fit checker set it from your product.

### Do I need the exact cutouts listed?
No. Search the Artwork tab for any similar cutout, or use your own logo. The ideas are prompts.

### Is it free?
Yes. The designer has no sign-up and no locked features.

### Can I order a box like this from Vayu?
Yes. Use **Send my design to Vayu** in the designer; the spec and a preview go with it. Prices are indicative until the quote is confirmed.
