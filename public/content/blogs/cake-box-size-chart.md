# Cake Box Size Chart: 0.5, 1 and 2 kg Cakes With Cake Board

Size a cake box from the board, not the cake: board width plus 2 cm clearance each side, and the tallest point plus 2 cm. An 8 inch board (20.3 cm) needs about 25 x 25 cm, a 6 inch board 20 x 20 cm and a 10 inch board 30 x 30 cm. Each opens in 3D below.

## The rule: board plus clearance

A cake sits on a board, and the board is what slides into the box. Decorated cakes are wider at the base than bare ones, and a topper makes them taller, so measure what you will actually pack.

1. **Length and width = board size + 2 x 2 cm.** That leaves 2 cm of clearance on every side so the cake does not scrape the walls.
2. **Height = tallest point of the cake + 2 cm.** Include the board thickness, any topper, candles and a high swirl of cream.
3. **Round up** to the next whole centimetre.

Worked example: an 8 inch board.

- Board: 8 x 2.54 = 20.32 cm.
- Add clearance: 20.32 + 4 = 24.32 cm, rounded up to **25 cm**.
- Cake and board stand 10 cm high with the topper: 10 + 2 = **12 cm**.
- Box inside size: **25 x 25 x 12 cm**, which is also the designer's one-kilo preset.

The 2 cm clearance is a rule of thumb, not a standard. A cake with a wide ruffle or piped border may need more, and a firm fondant cake less. Test with a sample.

## Choosing the window

The cake-box style has a window in the lid. The **window size slider** runs from 20 to 85% of the lid. A bigger window shows more cake, but leaves less board for your name and phone number and makes the lid less stiff, so a large box needs a strong enough board. For more on this trade-off, read [vents, hand holes and windows in box design](/blogs/vents-hand-holes-windows-box-design).

Put your bakery name, handle and phone number on the lid or a side wall, and keep the rest quiet. The [cutouts guide](/blogs/how-to-add-cutouts-box-design) shows how to add graphics, and [cake, sweet and bakery boxes](/blogs/cake-sweet-bakery-boxes) covers mithai boxes and trays.

## Height, boards and tiers

- **Cake boards vary in thickness.** A thicker cake drum adds to the height, so measure the cake together with its board.
- **Tall cakes** (tiered, with sugar flowers or tall toppers) need a deeper box. Measure the highest point, add 2 cm, and consider a separate topper that goes on at delivery.
- **Square cakes** use the same rule: board plus 4 cm each way.
- **Heat and cream.** In summer, cream cakes sit in cars. Ask your supplier about board options for warm or creamy products.

## Before you order: five checks

1. **Measure your boards.** Write down every size you use, in inches and cm, and keep to two or three.
2. **Measure the tallest cake** you sell, topper and board included, and set the box height for that one.
3. **Decide on the window.** A window shows the cake; a plain lid leaves more room for print. Switch it in the designer and look.
4. **Pack a real cake** in a sample, close the lid and carry it to a car or a bike. Check nothing touches the lid.
5. **Compare quantities.** The Price & order tab shows 100, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 boxes. The estimate is indicative, with a range of plus or minus 15%; the quote follows from the exact spec.

## A short note on food

Packaging that touches food needs extra care, and this article is **general guidance, not food-safety advice**. Moist or greasy cakes usually sit on a separate board or tray, and many bakers keep print on the outside and the food-contact surface plain. The designer helps with size, style and print. It does not certify any board, ink or film as food safe. Say exactly what you pack when you ask for a quote and confirm any food-safety rules with your own adviser.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size box do I need for a 1 kg cake?

A common 1 kg cake sits on an 8 inch (20.3 cm) board, which needs a box of about 25 x 25 cm and 12 cm high. Measure your own board and the tallest point of the cake, because recipes and decorations differ.

### How do I choose a box size for a cake?

Take the board size, add 2 cm on every side, and round up. For height, take the tallest point including the board and topper, and add 2 cm. For an 8 inch board that gives 25 x 25 cm, and 12 cm if the cake stands 10 cm high.

### Do I measure the cake or the cake board?

Measure the board. It is what goes into the box, and it is usually wider than the cake. Then measure the height of the cake and any topper, because that sets the box height.

### What size box for 6 cupcakes?

With 6 cm cups in a 3 x 2 grid, 1 cm gaps and 1 cm edges, the inside size is about 22 x 15 cm, and 9 cm high for cups with frosting. Measure your own cups first. A dozen in a 4 x 3 grid needs about 29 x 22 cm.

### Can I get a window cake box in a small quantity?

Our website currently states a minimum order of 500 boxes. For a home bakery, ask what other options exist before you decide, and compare 100, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 in the designer's indicative estimate.
