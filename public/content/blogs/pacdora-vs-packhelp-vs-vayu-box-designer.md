## How this comparison was made

We make boxes and we built one of these tools, so we are not neutral, and we say so up front. To keep the comparison fair we followed three rules:

1. **We describe only what each company says on its own public pages.** We did not buy a plan, test paid features or run the other tools' dielines through a cutter.
2. **We name what we do not know.** Where a page was silent (file formats, minimum orders, India), the table says "not stated on the pages we read" rather than guessing.
3. **We list where we are weaker.** A comparison that only has winners on one side is advertising.

If you are a vendor named here and something is out of date, [tell us](/contact) and we will correct it.

## Where Pacdora wins

- **Breadth.** Its pages cite 3,000+ dieline templates and 5,000+ mockups. We have 20 styles and 74 templates. If your box is unusual, they are more likely to have a starting shape.
- **File types.** DXF and AI downloads suit a die maker or designer who works in CAD. Our dielines are SVG and PDF; most design software opens SVG, but a CAD user may need a conversion.
- **Mockup variety.** If you need a bottle, a pouch and a box in one scene for a pitch deck, a mockup library is the right tool.

## Where Packhelp's Toolbox wins

- **Die-board layout.** An optimiser that fits die shapes on a sheet is a real production tool, and we do not have one.
- **Design and order in one place, if you live in its regions.** Its online editor shows a live 3D preview before ordering. The pages we read list European countries, the UK, the USA, Norway and Switzerland, so check availability before you plan around it from India.

## Where the Vayu Box Designer wins

- **Rupee estimate and a local quote path.** Set the size, board and quantity and see an indicative range in rupees, then send the same design to us. Estimates are indicative, not a quote; see [what drives the cost per box](/blogs/box-price-estimate-guide).
- **Indian shipping, food and retail styles.** Carry box with hand holes, ventilated produce carton, window cake box, takeaway clamshell, slide box and more, with sensible starting sizes. See [which box style to choose](/blogs/which-box-style-should-you-choose).
- **Strength in plain words.** Enter the packed weight and read a verdict on stack height. It is a simplified McKee-style estimate, so test a sample for anything heavy ([stacking guide](/blogs/box-stacking-strength-guide)).
- **Cutouts and presentation.** 512 ready cutouts you can tint to a brand colour, plus a presentation mode for showing a customer ([cutouts guide](/blogs/how-to-add-cutouts-box-design)).
- **Inside-size thinking.** The tool and our articles use inside dimensions, the way the corrugated trade quotes them.

## Where we are weaker

- No DXF or AI export and no die-board layout.
- Sizes are limited to 5 to 100 cm a side.
- Strength is an estimate, not a test result.

## A ten-minute workflow that uses more than one tool

You do not have to pick one. A sensible sequence for an Indian small business:

1. **Size the box** in the Vayu designer with the fit checker, and read the strength note.
2. **Download the SVG or PDF dieline** and hand it to your designer ([dielines explained](/blogs/dielines-explained)).
3. **Make a mockup elsewhere** if you want a lifestyle image for social posts that our product shots do not give you.
4. **Send the spec sheet** to two or three box makers and compare like for like. Our guide to [choosing a manufacturer in Ahmedabad](/blogs/choose-corrugated-box-manufacturer-ahmedabad) lists the questions.

Whichever tool made the dieline, the box maker will adjust allowances for their own machines. The inside size is what must not change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Pacdora free?

Its dieline generator page says you can create and customise a dieline for free, and it also mentions premium services with a separate pricing page. What is free and what is paid can change, so read the current pricing page before you plan around it.

### Is there a free Pacdora alternative for Indian businesses?

The Vayu Box Designer is free, needs no sign-up and works in rupees. It has fewer templates than Pacdora and exports SVG and PDF rather than DXF or AI, so choose by the file types and styles you need.

### Does Packhelp deliver to India?

The pages we read do not name India: the region switcher lists European countries, the UK, the USA, Norway and Switzerland. Ask Packhelp directly before planning an order. We cannot speak for their service.

### Can I use a dieline from one tool with another printer?

Usually yes, as a starting point, but the printer will confirm allowances for their own board and machines. Our dielines are yours to use anywhere. Always state the inside size you need.

### Which tool gives the most accurate dieline?

We cannot say, and we would not trust anyone who claims it. Accuracy depends on the board, the flute and the maker's machines. Treat any free dieline as a starting point and confirm it with the box maker.
