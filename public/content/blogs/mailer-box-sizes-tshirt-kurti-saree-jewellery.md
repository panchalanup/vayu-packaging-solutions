# Mailer Box Sizes for T-Shirts, Kurtis, Sarees and Jewellery

Pick a mailer by the product's folded size: add 1 cm padding on each side and 0.3 cm ease, then round up to 0.5 cm. A T-shirt folded to 28 x 22 x 3 cm needs about 30.5 x 24.5 x 5.5 cm inside. The table covers T-shirts, kurtis, sarees, jewellery and skincare.

## The rule behind every row

The 3D designer's fit checker uses three numbers, and the table follows them:

1. **Padding:** 1 cm on each side for normal goods (2.5 cm for fragile ones).
2. **Ease:** 0.3 cm in total, so the packed item slides in without force.
3. **Round up** each side to the next 0.5 cm.

So each side of the box is `folded size + 2 + 0.3`, rounded up. For a 28 cm long folded T-shirt: 28 + 2 + 0.3 = 30.3, rounded up to 30.5 cm. For the height of 3 cm: 3 + 2 + 0.3 = 5.3, rounded up to 5.5 cm.

Soft goods squash a little, so you may get away with less padding; fragile goods need more. Try your own numbers in the [box size calculator](/tools/box-size-calculator) and test a sample.

## How folding changes the size

The folded size is the biggest lever you control.

- **T-shirts and tees.** The thickness is the part that changes with the fold and the fabric. Two T-shirts at 3 cm each is 6 cm, so height goes from 5.5 to 8.5 cm in the table.
- **Kurtis.** A longer garment is usually folded in three, which makes a longer, narrower pack. Fold the same kurti two ways and measure both; the box follows the longest side.
- **Sarees.** A saree can be folded many ways, with the border showing or hidden. Fold it the way you want the customer to see it, and measure that pack. Add the blouse piece and any tissue paper to the thickness.
- **Jewellery.** Most pieces arrive in their own inner box or pouch. Size the mailer to that inner pack, not the jewellery. The designer does not model inserts or padding, so mention them when you ask for a quote.
- **Skincare.** Pack the items together as they will sit and measure the group. Bottles and jars do not squash, so keep the 1 cm padding and consider 2.5 cm if the items are glass.

For a single product, order a small number of boxes, pack real stock and give one to a friend to receive by courier before you place a large order.

## Board and weight

The mailer and window mailer presets in the designer use **3-ply with E flute**, which is thin and prints cleanly, and the links in the table use the same board. It suits clothes, kurtis and light skincare packs. For a heavy kit or glass bottles, try 5-ply and read the strength guidance in the designer. Our [types of corrugated boxes](/blogs/types-of-corrugated-boxes) guide explains 3, 5 and 7-ply.

A flat mailer also keeps volumetric weight down. At a divisor of 5000:

- One T-shirt in a 30.5 x 24.5 x 5.5 cm mailer: 4,110 cm3 / 5,000 = **0.82 kg**.
- The same T-shirt in a 30 x 20 x 15 cm shipper: 9,000 cm3 / 5,000 = **1.80 kg**.

That is almost 1 kg less billed weight (1.80 - 0.82 = 0.98 kg), as long as the packed T-shirt weighs less than 0.82 kg. The sums use inside sizes; add the board thickness for what a courier measures.

## Before you order: five checks

1. **Measure ten pieces** of the product, folded the way you will pack them, and use the largest.
2. **Decide the fold** and keep it. A different fold next month means a different box.
3. **Pack three real orders**: product, tissue or card, and the invoice. Add them to the height.
4. **Close it and shake it.** The product should not slide, and the lid should shut without force.
5. **Send one to a friend** by courier and ask how it arrived.

Then compare 100, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 boxes in the Price & order tab. The estimate is indicative, with a range of plus or minus 15%, and a quote follows from the exact spec.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size mailer box do I need for a T-shirt?

For a T-shirt folded to about 28 x 22 x 3 cm, an inside size of 30.5 x 24.5 x 5.5 cm is a good starting point. Measure your own folded T-shirt, add 1 cm of padding on each side and 0.3 cm of ease, and round up to 0.5 cm.

### Can one mailer size fit T-shirts and kurtis?

Sometimes. A mailer sized for the longest and widest product can hold the smaller one, but the extra space adds volumetric weight. If most of your orders are one product, order a box for that product.

### Should the size be inside or outside?

Ask for inside size, which is the space for the product. A courier measures the outside of the finished parcel, which is bigger by the board thickness on each side, so use the outside when you calculate shipping weight.

### Which mailer style is best for jewellery?

A small telescope box (lid and base) in the table suits a jewellery box that arrives with its own inner pack. For courier, it usually travels better inside a mailer or an outer carton. Mention inserts when you ask for a quote.

### What material should I choose for a mailer?

The designer's mailer presets use 3-ply E flute, which is thin and prints well. For heavy kits or glass items, ask about 5-ply and test a packed sample before ordering more.
