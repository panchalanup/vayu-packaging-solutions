# Packing Kesar Mangoes for Online Sale: 3, 5 and 10 kg

Size a mango box from the fruit count, not the weight. At an assumed 250 g a fruit, 5 kg is 20 fruit in two layers, which needs a box of about 41 x 23 x 17 cm inside, with vents and hand holes. The chart also covers 3 kg and 10 kg.

## Courier-ready or trader-ready?

The same fruit needs a different box depending on where it is going.

| | Trader-ready (to a mandi, wholesaler or by truck) | Courier-ready (online, to a home) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pack size** | Often 5 or 10 kg | 3 or 5 kg, light enough to carry from the door |
| **Box** | Ventilated carton or open tray, hand holes | Sealed carton with vents, hand holes optional |
| **Board** | 5-ply, stacked on pallets | 5-ply for 5 kg, 3-ply B or 5-ply for 3 kg |
| **Fruit** | Often packed in layers with paper between | Each fruit wrapped or in a pulp tray |
| **Main risk** | Crush under the stack, heat | Handling, time in transit, heat |
| **Label** | Variety, grade, weight, farm | Address, "this side up", brand |

A courier network is not a cold chain, and transit times vary. Check your courier's own terms for perishable goods before you promise delivery dates. This article does not state any courier, platform or export rule.

## How the sizes are worked out

The method is the one a packer uses: lay the fruit in a grid, add slack, check the weight.

1. **Weigh a sample of fruit.** Kesar mangoes vary by grade and by season, so weigh at least ten and take the average. We assumed **250 g**.
2. **Count the fruit for the pack.** 3 kg / 0.25 = 12, 5 kg / 0.25 = 20, 10 kg / 0.25 = 40.
3. **Measure one wrapped fruit lying down.** We assumed a cell of **11 cm long x 8 cm wide x 8 cm high**, which includes paper, net or a pulp tray.
4. **Stand the fruit across the box** so the 8 cm width runs along the box length and the 11 cm length runs across it. Two layers keep the box low and the weight per layer small.
5. **Add 1 cm of slack** to each direction.

Worked example, 5 kg: 20 fruit in 2 layers of 10, laid 5 along and 2 across.

- Length: 5 x 8 = 40, plus 1 = **41 cm**.
- Width: 2 x 11 = 22, plus 1 = **23 cm**.
- Height: 2 layers x 8 = 16, plus 1 = **17 cm**.
- Inside size: **41 x 23 x 17 cm**.

For fruit, the actual weight is higher than the volumetric weight in every row (the fruit alone is 3, 5 or 10 kg), so the billed weight is set by the packed weight, not the box volume. Fruit is a dense load.

The 10 kg box is wide and flat on purpose. With only two layers, each fruit in the bottom layer carries the weight of one layer above it instead of three, and the air has a shorter path through the box. The taller version has a smaller footprint but puts more weight on the bottom layer, so test it before you choose it. ## Vents and hand holes

Mangoes ripen and give off heat, so they need air. Vent slots let air through the box, and hand holes let people lift a full carton. Both cut into the walls.

- **Strength cost.** The designer's strength guidance warns that vents and hand holes often reduce stacking strength by about 10 to 40%, depending on size and position. Look at the strength panel with your box, and test a packed sample.
- **Vent area.** Cooling gains level off past about 8% of the wall area, so more slots are not always better.
- **Put the hand holes in the short walls.** That is where the designer places them, and it keeps the long walls strong.
- **Do not tape over the vents.** Seal along the top seam only.

Read [ventilated boxes for fruit and vegetables](/blogs/fruit-vegetable-ventilated-boxes) and [vents, hand holes and windows](/blogs/vents-hand-holes-windows-box-design) for the trade-off in detail.

## Board: 3-ply or 5-ply?

In the designer's guidance, 3-ply is comfortable for contents up to about 8 kg and 5-ply up to about 20 kg. Vents, hand holes, humid fruit rooms and stacking all reduce that margin.

- **3 kg courier pack:** 3-ply B can work for a single carton, and 5-ply gives a safer margin if parcels are stacked or the route is long. Test both.
- **5 kg and 10 kg:** start with 5-ply B + C, as the designer's mango template does.
- **Stacked on pallets:** use the strength panel for an indicative stack height, and keep cartons inside the pallet edge.

Board loses strength in damp air, so a carton that is fine in a dry shop may soften in a cold room or after sweating fruit. Ask about moisture if that is your route. See [how many kg a corrugated box can hold](/blogs/how-many-kg-can-corrugated-box-hold).

## Inside the box

The designer models the carton, not what sits in it. These are quote items, so mention them when you ask:

- **Paper wrap or a net sleeve** around each fruit, so fruit does not touch.
- **A pulp or paper tray** for each layer.
- **A sheet of paper** between layers, and a thin pad on the bottom.
- **Newspaper or shredded paper** is often used for filling, but loose filler can block vents and hold moisture.

Mangoes are food. Keep what touches the fruit clean and dry, and ask your supplier what documents come with the board and any paper or tray. We do not claim a food-safety certificate here.

## Branding the carton

A farm or brand name is part of the sale, especially online. In the designer, add your name on the long wall and a mango graphic from the cutouts library. Keep a clear area for the label.

- Put the variety and net weight on the outside, in plain letters.
- Add "this side up" marks. The designer can print fragile and this-side-up icons.
- Keep print to one or two colours on kraft, which is cheap and looks natural.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size box is needed for 5 kg of mangoes?

It depends on the fruit. At an assumed 250 g a fruit, 20 fruit in two layers of 5 x 2 need about 41 x 23 x 17 cm inside. The designer's own mango template is 36 x 26 x 14 cm. Weigh and measure your fruit and use the method above.

### How many Kesar mangoes fit in a 5 kg box?

About 20, if each fruit weighs about 250 g. Kesar mangoes vary by grade and season, so a 5 kg box can hold from fewer large fruit to more small ones. Weigh ten fruit and divide.

### Should a mango box have vents?

Usually yes, because fruit needs air and heat must escape. Vents and hand holes reduce stacking strength by roughly 10 to 40%, so keep them modest and test a packed carton.

### Which board is best for mango boxes?

5-ply B + C is a common starting point for 5 and 10 kg cartons, as in the designer's mango template. A 3 kg pack may work in 3-ply B. Test a sample with fruit, because vents and humidity lower the margin.

### Can I send mangoes by courier?

Many sellers do, but it depends on your courier's terms, the transit time and the weather. Check the courier's own rules for perishables, wrap each fruit and use a sealed, vented carton with a label on the top face.
