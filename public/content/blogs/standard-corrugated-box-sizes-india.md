# Standard Corrugated Box Sizes in India: Common Sizes Chart

Corrugated box sizes in India are set mostly by the product and the pallet, not one list. Common sizes run from 20 x 15 x 10 cm (small parcels) to 50 x 40 x 40 cm (heavy cartons). The chart has 12 example inside sizes in cm and inches, each opening in 3D. They are examples, not our stock list.

## How to read the chart

- **Inside size.** Every size is the **inside** length x width x height, the way the trade and the 3D designer quote it. Outside is bigger by the board thickness on each side; see [inside versus outside size](/blogs/box-measurements-guide).
- **Length is the longest side**, then width, then height.
- **Litres** is volume in cm3 divided by 1,000, so you can feel how roomy a box is. 9,000 cm3 is 9 litres.
- **Inches** are cm divided by 2.54, rounded to one decimal. Many Indian buyers quote inches, so both are shown.
- **Style** is the plain shipping carton (RSC) except where noted.

We are not aware of one official list that every maker follows, so this chart is a set of examples built from sizes people ask for, from round numbers and from the presets in our designer.

## Which ply goes with which size?

Size and weight are separate questions: a big box of pillows is light, and a small box of tools is heavy. Use the weight of the packed contents, not the size, to pick the board.

| Board | Wall | Comfortable contents (indicative) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-ply | Single wall | up to about 8 kg | Light goods, mailers, retail cartons |
| 5-ply | Double wall | up to about 20 kg | General shipping, produce, heavier goods |
| 7-ply | Triple wall | up to about 40 kg | Heavy and export cartons |

These weights are a planning guide, not a promise, and the same 8, 20 and 40 kg guide appears in [types of corrugated boxes](/blogs/types-of-corrugated-boxes). For anything heavy or stacked, check the [stacking strength guide](/blogs/box-stacking-strength-guide) and test a sample.

## Work out your own size in four steps

If nothing on the chart fits, the right size is the one your product needs.

1. **Measure the product** as it will lie in the box: length, width, height. Measure the biggest unit if sizes vary.
2. **Add padding on every side.** The designer's fit checker uses 1 cm per side, or 2.5 cm for fragile goods, plus 0.3 cm of ease so the product slides in. Round up to the next 0.5 cm.
3. **Compare with the chart.** Take the nearest size that is at least as big in every direction.
4. **Check what the extra space costs.** Empty space raises courier and freight cost.

Worked example: a product 24 x 16 x 7 cm with default padding.

- Length: 24 + 2 + 0.3 = 26.3, rounded up to **26.5 cm**.
- Width: 16 + 2 + 0.3 = 18.3, rounded up to **18.5 cm**.
- Height: 7 + 2 + 0.3 = 9.3, rounded up to **9.5 cm**.
- Inside size: **26.5 x 18.5 x 9.5 cm**, a volume of 4,657 cm3.

The nearest chart size that fits is 30 x 20 x 15 cm, which is 9,000 cm3. At a divisor of 5000, that is 1.80 kg of volumetric weight against 0.93 kg for the exact size. The exact size has to be made to order and may need its own tooling, so ask what applies; see [how the estimate works](/blogs/box-price-estimate-guide). The [volumetric weight article](/blogs/volumetric-weight-calculator-india) explains the divisor.

## Sizes that fit a pallet without waste

If you ship by pallet, pick a footprint that divides a standard pallet. The calculation is plain division. On a 120 x 100 cm pallet footprint, with every carton the same way round:

| Carton footprint (cm) | Along 120 cm | Along 100 cm | Per layer | Floor left over |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 x 50 | 2 | 2 | 4 | none |
| 40 x 25 | 3 | 4 | 12 | none |
| 30 x 25 | 4 | 4 | 16 | none |
| 24 x 20 | 5 | 5 | 25 | none |

A footprint that does not divide the pallet leaves gaps. A 45 x 35 cm carton gives 2 x 2 = 4 per layer in a simple grid. A mixed layout with some cartons turned can reach 6 per layer, which covers 6 x 45 x 35 = 9,450 of 12,000 cm2, so about 21% of the floor stays empty. If your product allows it, nudging a side by a centimetre or two can close the gap.

These are outside sizes of the packed carton, and the pallet size in common use is a typical figure, so confirm it with your carrier. The [CBM and container article](/blogs/cbm-calculator-cartons-per-container) works through pallets and containers.

## Standard size or custom size?

A common size is easy to compare between suppliers and may be quicker to arrange. A custom size removes empty space, which can lower shipping cost and looks better when the product fits snugly. For a small run, the decision usually depends on setup cost and minimum quantity, so ask for both quotes.

Use a common size when:

- you sell many different products and want a few boxes to cover them;
- the product is small or irregular and you are testing demand;
- you are comparing quotes and want the same size from every supplier.

Choose a custom size when:

- one product accounts for most shipments;
- you pay courier freight on volumetric weight;
- the box is part of your brand and a snug fit matters.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the standard size of a corrugated box?

There is no single standard size. Boxes are made to fit the product, and buyers tend to work from common sizes such as 30 x 20 x 15 cm for small parcels and 50 x 40 x 40 cm for heavy cartons. Use the chart above as a starting point, not a rule.

### Are box sizes inside or outside dimensions?

Box makers normally quote inside size, which is the space for your product. The outside size is bigger by the board thickness on each side. Always say "inside" or "outside" when you order, because mixing them up is a common cause of boxes that do not fit.

### How do I convert box size from inches to cm?

Multiply inches by 2.54. For example, 12 inches x 2.54 = 30.48 cm, and 8 inches x 2.54 = 20.32 cm. The designer shows mm, cm and inches, so you can type either.

### Which box size is best for shipping?

The best size is the smallest one that holds the product with enough padding. A box that is too big costs more to ship and lets the product move; a box that is too small can crush it. Use the fit checker and test one packed sample.

### Can I order a size that is not on the chart?

Yes, you can design any inside size between 5 and 100 cm in the designer, then ask for a quote. Ask your supplier about the minimum order and whether a new size needs its own tooling.
