# Festive Gift and Hamper Boxes for Diwali and Weddings

In India, a gift is judged before it is opened. A box of sweets for Diwali, a wedding hamper for guests, or a corporate gift for clients all say something about the person giving it, and the box is the first thing anyone sees.

The classic answer is the **telescope box**: a base and a separate lid that slides over it. It looks premium, the lid lifts off in one satisfying movement, and it is easy to fill with sweets, dry fruit, candles or a curated hamper. This guide shows how to plan and design one in the free **3D Box Designer**, and how to avoid the last-minute rush that catches so many festive orders.

## The problem: festive orders run on tight deadlines

Festive and wedding packaging has its own challenges:

- **Fixed dates.** Diwali, a wedding or a company's annual event will not move because your boxes are late.
- **Many approvers.** Families, event planners and HR or admin teams all want a say in the design.
- **Mixed contents.** Hampers combine jars, boxes, bottles and loose items, which makes sizing tricky.
- **High expectations.** A gift box is expected to look rich, even if the budget is modest.
- **Busy suppliers.** Many packaging suppliers are at their busiest before major festivals.

## Why telescope (lid-and-base) boxes work for gifts

- **The reveal.** Lifting a lid straight up feels more special than tearing open flaps.
- **No tape on the design.** The lid holds by friction, so the outside stays clean.
- **Flexible depth.** A shallow lid is economical; a full-depth lid that covers the whole base feels luxurious and adds strength.
- **Reusable.** Recipients often keep good gift boxes for storage, which keeps your brand or family name in the home.
- **Easy to fill.** The open base takes trays, inserts, shred or tissue easily.

## Planning your gift box

### Decide what goes in

List every item: a 500 g sweet box, two jars, a candle, a card. Measure each, then arrange them on a table the way you want them to sit. Measure the footprint of the arrangement and the tallest item. This becomes the starting inside size of the base.

### Decide the feel

| Feel | Board and finish ideas |
|---|---|
| Traditional, warm | Rich red, maroon or deep green outside; a gold-coloured ribbon; kraft or cream inside |
| Modern, minimal | White or black outside, one accent colour, matte finish |
| Natural, eco | Natural kraft outside and inside, a simple stamp-style logo |
| Corporate | Company colour outside, logo on the lid, a short message inside |

### Plan the inside

Hampers often need **inserts or trays** to stop items moving. Inserts are not designed in the tool yet, but you can use the cut-away view to look inside the base and judge how much space you have for them. Mention inserts in your enquiry.

## Step by step: designing a telescope gift box

### 1. Open the gift box preset

Go to the [gift box designer](/box-designer/gift-box). It loads the telescope style: a base with a slightly larger lid.

### 2. Set the base size

Enter the inside length, width and height of the base from your planning. Add a little clearance for tissue, shred or an insert.

### 3. Set the lid depth

Choose how far the lid comes down. A shallow lid of a few centimetres is economical. A full-depth lid covers the base entirely. Try both and watch the fold animation lift the lid off.

### 4. Choose colours and finish

Pick a board colour for the outside and set an inside colour. Enter your exact brand or theme colour as a hex or RGB value if you have one. Choose matte for a soft, premium look or gloss for shine.

![The brand colour picker with a hex input and recent colours](/blog-images/designer/designer-brand-colours.png)

### 5. Add the logo, name or message

On the lid: a family monogram, a couple's initials, a company logo or a festive greeting. On the inside of the lid: a short message such as "With warm wishes this Diwali". Keep important text away from the edges; the print checks warn you if something is too close to a fold.

### 6. Make a few variants

Save two or three versions (for example red with gold text, cream with red text, kraft with a printed logo) as separate designs. Switch between them in presentation mode when showing the family or the client.

### 7. Share for approval

Send a share link to everyone who needs to approve it, or download the **spec sheet PDF** with three views, size, board, finish, quantity and an approval block. For corporate orders, the PDF is easy to attach to an internal purchase request.

### 8. Get a price early

Check the indicative estimate across quantities, then send the design to us for a firm quote. The earlier you send it, the more options you have.

## Ideas for each occasion

### Diwali and festive gifting

- Deep festive colours with a single metallic-look accent.
- A short greeting inside the lid.
- Sizes planned around standard sweet and dry-fruit boxes, so one gift box suits several hampers.

### Weddings

- Initials or a monogram on the lid, wedding date on the side.
- Colours matched to the wedding theme.
- A smaller box for guest favours and a larger one for family hampers, in the same design.

### Corporate gifting

- Company logo on the lid, one brand colour.
- A message from the leadership team inside.
- A sturdy board, because corporate gifts are often couriered to many cities.
- Plain outer shipping cartons so the gift box arrives clean.

## Tips to avoid festive-season stress

- **Start early.** Design and approve before the rush. Final quantities can follow.
- **Lock the size first.** Size affects the die, the board and the price. Colours and text are easier to change later.
- **Use one base size for several hampers** where you can. It simplifies production and storage.
- **Confirm colours on a proof.** Screen colours are approximate, especially on kraft board.
- **Plan for delivery.** If gifts are couriered, design an outer carton too.
- **Keep the design files together.** My designs and the brand kit keep every version and your logo in one place in your browser.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I match my wedding or brand colour?

You can enter any hex or RGB colour in the designer to preview it. Final printed colours depend on board and print method, so confirm them on a physical proof.

### Can you make inserts for hampers?

Inserts are not part of the designer yet. Mention them in your enquiry with the items you are packing, and we will discuss options.

### How early should I order festive boxes?

As early as you can. Suppliers are busy before major festivals. Design and approve early, then confirm quantities.

### Is the gift box strong enough to courier?

It depends on the board and the contents. For couriered gifts, we usually suggest a plain outer carton. The designer's strength guidance gives an indicative verdict for your size and board.

### Can I show the design to my family or client without them installing anything?

Yes. Send a share link; it opens in any browser on a phone or laptop.
