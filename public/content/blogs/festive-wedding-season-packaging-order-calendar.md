# Festive and Wedding Season Packaging Order Calendar

For printed custom boxes, start the order about 8 to 12 weeks before the date you need stock in hand, and work backwards from that date, not from the festival. This calendar lists the occasions that usually drive box orders, month by month, with when to start. Festival dates move yearly, so check this year's.

## How to use the calendar

1. Find the date you need your boxes **in hand**, which is usually a week or two before you start selling, not the festival day.
2. Count back using the planner below, or the 8 to 12 week rule.
3. Use the table to see which boxes to think about for that occasion and what to do first.

Many suppliers are busier before the big festivals, so the later you ask, the fewer options you may have. That is a general pattern, not a statement about any one supplier; ask yours.

## The backward planner

Work back from the day you need the boxes **in hand**. The stage times below are illustrative, not Vayu lead times; ask each supplier for theirs.

| Stage | Illustrative time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Slack for surprises | 10 days | Late artwork, a reprint, a delayed truck |
| Transit | 3 days | Supplier to you |
| Production | 14 days | Plates, die, printing and finishing |
| Sample or proof approval | 7 days | A physical sample or a signed proof |
| Artwork and proof | 7 days | Fix the artwork, run the print checks |
| Size and style decision | 7 days | Pick the size, board and style |

**Worked example.** You need 1,000 hamper boxes in hand on 1 November. The stages add up to 10 + 3 + 14 + 7 + 7 + 7 = 48 days. Counting back 48 days from 1 November gives 14 September. That is the day you should be ready to approve the size and style, not the day you place the order. If your supplier's production time is 21 days, you start a week earlier.

## What to settle first, whichever occasion

- **Size and style.** Choose the box for the contents, then decide on printing. For gift boxes see the [gift box page](/box-designer/gift-box); for sweets, a [cake or bakery style](/box-designer/cake-box) or a [carry box](/box-designer/carry-box) for hampers.
- **Print.** One brand colour is cheaper and quicker to proof than full colour ([printing on corrugated](/blogs/flexo-offset-digital-printing-corrugated-boxes)).
- **Quantity.** Our current minimum order is 500 boxes ([minimum order](/blogs/minimum-order-quantity-custom-boxes)). If you need fewer, ask about options.
- **Food contact.** For sweets and cakes, ask the maker what is verified for food contact before you put food directly in a box. Do not print "food grade" unless you hold the paperwork.
- **Generic festive artwork.** Use cutouts for lamps, stars, gifts and flowers, and keep the year off the box if you want leftovers to be reusable ([how to add cutouts](/blogs/how-to-add-cutouts-box-design)).
- **Reorders.** A one-colour design is simple to reorder mid-season if stock sells out; ask what a reorder costs and how long it takes.

## A one-page version to share

> **Festive packaging checklist.** 1. Write down the date boxes must be in hand. 2. Subtract 8 to 12 weeks. 3. Decide size, style, board and print. 4. Check the indicative estimate. 5. Send the spec and artwork for a quote. 6. Approve the sample or proof. 7. Confirm the delivery date in writing.

Print it, or photograph it and send it to whoever places the order.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting back from the festival** instead of from the day you need the stock.
- **Changing the size late.** A new size can mean a new die and a new proof.
- **Waiting for final artwork before asking for a quote.** Ask for a quote on size, board and colours; send artwork in parallel.
- **No slack.** A reprint or a delay at festival time can cost the whole season.
- **Ordering the whole season in one go.** A smaller first order plus a quick reorder may be safer for a new design, if your supplier can support it.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I order Diwali gift boxes?

Start about 8 to 12 weeks before you need the boxes in hand, which for a November festival usually means August or early September. Check this year's Diwali date and your supplier's actual lead time.

### How early should I order wedding packaging?

The main wedding months vary by region and year, and the box supplier's time is the same as for any printed box. Plan from the date your stock must be ready, using the backward planner, and ask your supplier for a firm lead time.

### Can I order festive boxes at short notice?

Sometimes, in simple designs or sizes a supplier already makes. It depends on the supplier and their queue. Ask before the rush, and use a one-colour design to keep proofing quick.

### Do festive boxes cost more?

Prices follow size, board, print and quantity, not the festival. Paper prices and supplier demand can change over the year, so ask for a current quote. The designer gives an indicative range only.

### Is the calendar the same every year?

The seasons are, but festival and wedding dates move with the calendar, so check this year's dates and adjust. We update this page each year.
