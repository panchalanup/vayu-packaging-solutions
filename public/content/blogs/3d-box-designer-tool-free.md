# Stop Wasting Money on Wrong-Sized Boxes: Design Perfect Packaging in 3D Before You Order

A box that is 2 cm too short is not 2 cm of a problem. It is a whole run of boxes you cannot use, a reorder at short notice, and a launch that waits for packaging.

Most wrong boxes are not caused by bad factories. They are caused by **descriptions**: a size typed into an email without saying inside or outside, a logo "on the front" that ends up under a flap, a "strong box" that turns out to be 3-ply when the product needed 5-ply. Everyone did their job; the picture in each person's head was different.

That is the problem the free **3D Box Designer** solves. You build the box on screen, exactly as it will be made, and everybody looks at the same picture.

## Why packaging orders go wrong

### "Is that inside or outside?"
Corrugated board has thickness: roughly 3-4 mm for 3-ply and 6-7 mm for 5-ply. A box ordered by its outside size comes out smaller inside than you expect. Quote inside dimensions and the box maker adds the allowance; mix them up and the product does not fit. Our [box measurement guide](/blogs/box-measurements-guide) explains this in detail.

### "Which way is length?"
300 × 200 × 150 and 300 × 150 × 200 are completely different boxes. One opens wide and shallow, the other narrow and deep.

### "Put the logo on the front"
On a flat drawing, the front panel looks like a clean rectangle. On the real box, flaps fold over its top edge, and tape runs across the top. Artwork placed without folding the box often ends up partly hidden.

### "Make it strong"
Strong for 2 kg in a courier bag and strong for 20 kg at the bottom of a pallet are different boxes. Without a weight and a stacking plan, "strong" means different things to different people.

### "Roughly how much?"
Without a spec, a supplier cannot give a meaningful price, and without a price you cannot decide on the spec. So the emails go back and forth.

Every one of these is easier when you can **see** the box.

## What the 3D Box Designer does

The designer runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

### Eight box styles, in real 3D
Regular slotted carton (RSC), half slotted (HSC), full overlap, sleeve, telescope lid and base, mailer, pizza box and tray. Each one folds on screen from a flat blank to a sealed box, so you see how it opens and where every flap goes. Not sure which to choose? Read [8 box styles in 3D](/blogs/box-styles-in-3d), or let **Find my box** recommend one from a few questions about your product.

### The right size, in your units
Enter **inside** dimensions in **mm, cm or inches**; the tool adds the board thickness and shows the outside size. The **fit checker** works out the inside size from your product's size, the padding you want and whether it is fragile. See [how to find the right box size](/blogs/find-right-box-size).

### Board, colour and finish
Choose 3, 5 or 7-ply and the flute, natural kraft, white or a printed colour, a different inside colour, and a matte or gloss finish. The board edge on the model gets visibly thicker as you add plies.

### Your logo, checked for print
Upload your logo (SVG, PNG, JPG or WebP), add text, set exact brand colours, and place them on any face, including inside surfaces such as a mailer lid. **Print checks** flag low-resolution images, artwork too close to a fold or over an edge, and text that is too small or hard to read. See [print-ready logo tips](/blogs/logo-artwork-on-boxes).

### Strength in plain words
Enter the weight of one packed box and the designer tells you whether the board is comfortable and roughly how high you can stack, for example *"Good for up to ~15 kg per box, stack 6 high"*, and suggests a stronger ply if needed. See [will my box stack?](/blogs/box-stacking-strength-guide)

### An instant, indicative price
See an estimated price per box and for the order at 100, 500, 1,000 or 5,000 boxes (or any quantity you type), shown as a range and updated as you change the design. It is a guide, not a quote; the final price depends on paper grades and current paper rates. See [what drives cost per box](/blogs/box-price-estimate-guide).

### Delivery time by pincode
Enter a city or pincode for an indicative number of days, combining production time and transit.

### Everything you need to hand over
- a **share link** that opens your exact design,
- a **dieline** (SVG or PDF, 1:1 mm) for your designer or any printer: [dielines explained](/blogs/dielines-explained),
- a **spec sheet / proof PDF** with three views, the full spec and a customer approval block,
- **product shots and 360° or fold videos** for launches and decks: [product shots without a photoshoot](/blogs/packaging-product-shots-360-video),
- a **3D model** (GLB) and an AR view on iPhone and iPad.

### Saved in your browser
**My designs** keeps versions of your designs in your browser, and a **brand kit** remembers your logo and colours. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send or share it.
